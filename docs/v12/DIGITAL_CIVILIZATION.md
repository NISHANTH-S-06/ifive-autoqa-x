## Core Capabilities

### 1. Digital Civilization Core

- **CivilizationEngine**: Master orchestrator governing civilization epochs and policy compliance audits.
- **CivilizationManager**: Manages civilization demographic metrics, epoch transitions, and health indices.
- **GlobalGovernance**: Enforces fundamental constitutional AI laws, policy proposals, and voting consensus.

### 2. Global AI Society

- **AgentSociety**: Coordinates agent population interactions, guild standings, and reputation scores.
- **CommunityManager**: Manages specialized AI guilds (Engineering, Testing, Security, Governance).
- **CollaborationNetwork**: Manages inter-guild communication channels and graph topology.

### 3. Autonomous Economy

- **DigitalEconomy**: Economic balance and marketplace orchestrator.
- **ResourceExchange**: Tokenized trading of compute credits, bandwidth tokens, and model inference rights.
- **ValueEngine**: Calculates contribution value scores, issues reward credits, and maintains market equilibrium.

### 4. Global Coordination

- **PlanetCoordinator**: Planetary-scale supervisor for multi-region system health and resource scheduling.
- **GlobalScheduler**: Time-zone and green-energy grid aware task scheduler.
- **CrisisManager**: Emergency anomaly detection, containment protocols, and automated stabilization.
